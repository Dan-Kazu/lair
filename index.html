Mob presets

Click a preset, then click the map to drop it. Hold Shift to drop several. Esc cancels.

Initiative

Groups take one slot. Open a group to set who goes first inside it, and mark its infighting roll at the start of each round.

      Groups

      Select tokens on the map, then press New group or a group's Add selected. A group can mix any creatures.

      Encounters

      Drag a box on empty map space to select lots of mobs at once (hold Shift to add to the selection). Mobs can be in a group and in an encounter at the same time. Start adds the encounter's groups and solo mobs to initiative; End takes them back out.
